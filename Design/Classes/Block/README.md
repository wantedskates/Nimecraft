# Universal features 
Each block :- 
1. Has six faces and each face has a texture
2. Capable to be destroyed , put , interacted with or crafted
3. Releases a sound and visual effect when destroyed , put, crafted and interacted with
4. Release a sound when a creature jump and move on it
5. Some blocks have special behaviors under some conditions (e.x., a sponge changing its texture from wet -> dry or the opposite when put under water)
6. Have a behavior when put in a face of other and this behavior is different or the same for different faces for different faces
7. Capable of being grabbed by a creature and copies of it are stored in the inventory of the creature who grabbed it
